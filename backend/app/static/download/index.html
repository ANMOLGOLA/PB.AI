Assessment Instructions for Candidates

Follow these steps to complete your examination securely:

1
Download & Extract

Download the Sentinel Exam package or launcher script onto your Windows machine.

2
Launch Client

Double-click start-exam.bat. The app launches immediately into secure full-screen mode.

3
Share Entire Screen

On the pre-exam portal, click Share Entire Screen to initiate live transmission to the proctor.

4
Complete & Submit

Answer questions, review flagged items on the question palette, and submit before timer runs out.
